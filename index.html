About Me

I'm a BSc. student in Computer Science at BRAC University, driven by a strong passion for technology, innovation and problem-solving. Although I'm currently living in Dhaka, the vibrant capital of Bangladesh, but my roots lie in Khulna, one of the most peaceful and culturally rich cities in the country. My immediate goal is to successfully complete my BSc in Computer Science, building a solid foundation in software development, algorithms, and emerging technologies. Looking ahead, I aspire to pursue a Master’s degree abroad to deepen my expertise and gain global exposure. With long-term ambitions of working at leading tech companies such as Google, Amazon, Meta or Apple. I aim to refine my skills on a world-class stage and contribute to impactful, large-scale projects. Ultimately, after acquiring sufficient experience and resources, I plan to establish my own tech-driven company and create solutions that bring meaningful change. I am committed to continuous learning, personal growth and building a future where my skills can make a difference.
